\section*{Why I Am Interested in This Program}

My interest stems from a fascination with interoperability and the complex challenges of merging mature blockchain frameworks. Hyperledger Cacti represents the pinnacle of this challenge—integrating the legacy of Hyperledger Cactus and Weaver into a unified, high-performance interoperability engine. 

I am particularly motivated by the opportunity to improve the "developer experience" at a foundational level. Reducing CI/CD runtimes from 45 minutes to under 15 minutes is not just a technical optimization; it's a force multiplier for every contributor to the project. Similarly, restructuring fragmented documentation into a clear, tiered architecture (Level 1: Library, Level 2: API/REST, Level 3: Full Framework) directly lowers the entry barrier for the next generation of Cacti developers. Being part of the "Cleanup Initiative" allows me to contribute to the long-term sustainability and maintainability of a critical piece of decentralized infrastructure.

\section*{Experience and Knowledge/Skills Applicable to This Program}

My background in distributed systems, TypeScript/Node.js development, and DevOps aligns perfectly with the needs of the Cacti Cleanup project. Over the past few years, I have developed a strong proficiency in Docker-based orchestration and CI/CD optimization.

For this specific mentorship, I have already conducted significant preliminary research (available in the \texttt{researches/} directory of this workspace):
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{CI/CD Audit:} Identified critical bottlenecks in the current Cacti pipeline, such as redundant Docker builds and lack of \texttt{node\_modules} caching. I've proposed a parallelized execution strategy that can reduce build times by over 60\%.
    \item \textbf{Documentation Analysis:} Mapped the fragmentation between the "Node Server" (Cactus) and "Relay" (Weaver) architectures. I developed a proposal for a Tier-based documentation structure to unify these legacies.
    \item \textbf{Codebase Understanding:} Navigated the monorepo structure and identified areas where dependency management and build-cache mechanisms (\texttt{.tsbuildinfo}) can be improved to speed up local and remote development.
\end{itemize}

My hands-on experience with TypeScript, combined with a deep dive into Cacti's architecture, ensures I can contribute effectively to both documentation restructuring and technical infrastructure improvements.
